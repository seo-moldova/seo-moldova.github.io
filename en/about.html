Personal

A little beyond the work

I’m Ivan Pridorojnov, an SEO specialist from Bălți. This page is a little about life outside my projects: books, psychology, technology and the cat usually somewhere near my desk.

01
Origins

Bălți, books and curiosity

I was born and raised in Bălți. I’ve always liked understanding how things work, whether it’s a new technology or a different way of looking at something familiar. That curiosity has stayed with me in my work.

I read science fiction and fantasy, follow technology and enjoy specialist books. I like a book that stays with you after the last page.

“
Search for Meaning

Psychology and philosophy

Analytical psychology and philosophy are another interest. I’m curious about how people understand themselves, what shapes their decisions and how different writers make sense of that experience.

I read for my own interest, without looking for persuasion techniques or another marketing framework. I enjoy a conversation where a good question doesn’t have an easy answer.

That habit helps at work too. Before suggesting a solution, I try to understand the situation and listen to the person I’m working with.

03
Family

A cat and a home office

I share my home with a cat. He has his own routine, his own character and a firm opinion about when I should step away from the screen.

Sometimes he sleeps nearby; sometimes he wants attention. He’s good company when I’m working from home, and a convincing reason to take a break.

04
Harmony

Time away from the screen

I value time away from the desk. After a day in front of a screen, even an ordinary walk helps me switch gears.

I take a relaxed approach to exercise. For me, it’s about moving, feeling better and coming back to work with a clearer head.

I like understanding things, reading and doing work I can take personal responsibility for.

If that sounds like someone you’d like to work with, I’d be glad to hear about your project.
