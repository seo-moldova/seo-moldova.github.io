Common questions

When should SEO join development?

Before the structure, URLs and templates are approved. Architecture becomes more expensive to correct later.

Can a migration have zero traffic loss?

Zero volatility cannot be promised, but redirect planning, validation and monitoring can materially reduce the risk.

What is needed from developers?

Access to the URL map, templates, staging environment and an owner for implementation.
