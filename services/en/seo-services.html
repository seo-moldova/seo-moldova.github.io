05

Common questions

When should I expect results?

Timing depends on the website, competition and how quickly changes are made. You may see movement after Google recrawls the pages, but sustained growth usually takes longer.

Can you guarantee rankings?

No. Rankings also depend on competitors and Google algorithms. I am responsible for the analysis, recommendations, support and checks I provide.

How does reporting work?

We agree on a baseline and KPIs, then track implementation, visibility, traffic and conversions.

Do you write the website content too?

Yes. I research the topic and keywords, then write and edit service pages, category copy and articles. I’ll need accurate product information and your team’s help checking facts specific to the business.

How does SEO for an online store differ from a service website?

Stores usually have more categories, filters and product pages, so indexing and templates need particular attention. Service websites often start with demand, a clear offer and the right landing pages. The work depends on the individual website.
