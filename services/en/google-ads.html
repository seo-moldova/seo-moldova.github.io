How the engagement works

  1. 01

    Goals and budget

    We discuss the services to promote, target regions and languages, budget and target cost per lead.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    I research keywords, review landing pages and prepare ads and conversion measurement.

  3. 03

    Launch or audit

    I launch agreed campaigns or review the existing account and prepare corrections.

  4. 04

    Management

    I review search terms and lead quality, adjust bids and ads and test changes.

What you receive

New campaign setup

I choose keywords, write ads and set up targeting for the right regions. Before launch, I check that conversion tracking works.

Google Ads audit

I review structure, queries, negatives, ads, landing pages and conversions. You receive a report explaining the problems and which fixes to tackle first.

Ongoing management

I review budget use and search terms and test changes. With CRM access, I can compare reported conversions with lead quality.

What I need to get started

To start, I need the website, priority services, markets and languages, and access to the ad account and analytics. Setting up conversion tracking may require help from your developer or the person who manages your CRM.

Working together and pricing

Setup and audits are available as one-off projects. For monthly management, we agree which campaigns I will handle and how often I will report on them. We confirm the tasks and specialist fee before work starts.

Common questions

Can I order just an audit?

Yes. I review the account and prepare recommendations. Implementation or ongoing management can be agreed separately.

How do you evaluate advertising quality?

I review spend, conversions and lead quality. A button click and a confirmed enquiry are separate actions.

Can you guarantee a number of leads?

Results depend on demand, competition, the offer, budget and landing pages. We agree goals and measurement first, then evaluate them using actual data.

Do you only work in Moldova?

I work with businesses in Moldova and international projects. Markets, languages and scope are agreed for each engagement.
