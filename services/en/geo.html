Deliverables and working format

SEO, GEO and AEO

SEO supports page discovery in search. GEO focuses on visibility in generated answers. AEO emphasizes clear answers to specific questions. AI SEO is a broad term for applying SEO to AI search. These goals overlap and are considered together.

Website and expert content

I clarify services, company and author information, answer structure and evidence. Content should help people choose a solution and give sources accurate information.

External evidence

I review profile consistency and authentic mentions. Recommendations rely on verifiable facts, experience and accurate company information.

What I need to get started

I need the website, brand name and variants, services, target markets and languages, known competitors and public profiles. Search Console and analytics access can help verify page accessibility and referral visits.

Format and pricing

Start with a one-off GEO audit and a prioritized plan. Technical and content implementation is scoped after diagnosis. Cost depends on the number of markets, questions and pages.

What the audit records

For each question, the audit retains the system, date, conditions, answer and sources. Brand mentions, recommendations and links to your site are recorded separately.

05

Common questions

Can you guarantee a ChatGPT recommendation?

No. Answers depend on the system, question, sources and context. We can agree verifiable work and evaluation, but not a guaranteed recommendation.

Does Google AI need special markup?

Google states that AI Overviews and AI Mode do not require special AI markup. Standard search requirements and helpful, accessible content remain relevant.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. Indexation, accessibility and clear content remain the foundation. GEO adds analysis of answers and sources in selected AI systems.

Can we start with a small scope?

We choose one priority service, one market and a set of questions. The audit then determines which changes to implement first.
