What Was Done

A comprehensive audit identified technical, structural and backlink issues. The website architecture was reworked, new service landing pages were created, a content strategy was developed for specific lower-competition queries, and a PPC strategy was implemented.

Key work areas:

🔧

Technical Recovery

Spam link cleanup, speed optimization, duplicate removal, broken link fixes and JSON-LD implementation

🏗️

Architecture & Content

Site architecture restructuring, landing pages for each service, case studies section, FAQ page, commercial page optimization

📈

Promotion Strategy

Low-frequency, low-competition blog strategy, link building to bypass competitors, PPC campaigns for brand awareness

Google Analytics — Traffic Dynamics (2020-2023)

The site had lost organic visibility while carrying a large number of spammy backlinks and unresolved technical issues. After the audit and cleanup, organic traffic and rankings recovered. The available data does not isolate a single algorithmic cause.

Result: the site started with ~200 users per month (early 2020) and grew to 1,200–1,500 users by the end of 2022. Net growth was +650%.

Traffic remained low until mid-2021 and then entered a more consistent growth trend. The timing coincides with the implemented cleanup and content work, but the chart does not establish one algorithmic cause.
