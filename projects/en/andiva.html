SEO Case Study | Transition to Intensive Growth Phase

Andiva, MD

Category Recruitment / Job Portal
Period 2023 — 2024
Location Moldova

SEO work for a recruitment platform: I reorganised the structure, vacancy pages and technical settings. Over the period shown, average traffic rose by 150% and peak values by 300%.

Data note: metrics refer to the period and sources shown in this case study. Rankings, impressions and traffic alone do not establish the number of enquiries or sales.

🎯

Challenge

Conduct a complete structural and semantic optimization of an existing recruitment website, improve taxonomy and content organization, fix technical issues, and enhance usability, SEO performance, and job management system.

What Was Done

I reorganised categories and vacancy pages and updated the copy and metadata. Alongside that, I worked on URLs, indexing and structured data.

Key work areas:

🏗️

Structure & Taxonomy

Expansion of categories and subcategories, job card templates, improved navigation and logical site structure

📝

Content & On-Page

Rewriting and optimizing key pages, updating SEO tags, heading structure, adding alt texts

⚙️

Technical SEO

URL fixes, sitemap and robots.txt updates, JSON-LD implementation, canonical tags, security improvements

📋

Detailed Work List

Detailed work breakdown

Structure & Taxonomy

  • Expanded site structure with new categories and subcategories
  • Created job card templates
  • Developed templates for jobs and service pages
  • Improved navigation and logical site structure

Content & On-Page

  • Rewrote and optimized key pages
  • Updated SEO tags and meta descriptions
  • Optimized heading structure (H1, H2)
  • Added alt texts for images
  • Improved internal linking

Technical SEO

  • Fixed and optimized URLs
  • Updated Sitemap and Robots.txt
  • Added canonical tags
  • Implemented JSON-LD
  • Improved security and technical SEO settings
  • Competitor analysis and semantic development
