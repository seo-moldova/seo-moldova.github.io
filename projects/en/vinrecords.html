SEO Case Study | Launching a VIN Service

VinRecords.ca

Category Specialized Service / VIN Decoding
Period November 2025 — March 2026
Location Canada (.ca)

SEO launch for a VIN checking service in Canada. I researched keywords, planned the pages and prepared the website for search. Within five months, visibility reached 73.2K impressions and “car appraisal by vin” entered the top three.

Data note: metrics refer to the period and sources shown in this case study. Rankings, impressions and traffic alone do not establish the number of enquiries or sales.

🎯

Challenge

From scratch, capture search visibility for specific queries (VIN decoding, vehicle appraisal), ensuring organic traffic growth in a highly competitive environment with major aggregators in the Canadian market.
