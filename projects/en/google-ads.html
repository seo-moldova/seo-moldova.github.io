Google Ads — 11 advertising projects

ChannelGoogle Ads / Paid Search
PeriodNovember 2023 — November 2025
Scope11 advertising projects

Managing advertising campaigns for 11 projects in recruitment, transportation, Romanian citizenship and tax refunds. Over two years, all conversions increased by 259.53% while cost per conversion fell by 60.29%, with ad spend increasing by 42.79%.

🎯

Objective

Drive relevant traffic to projects serving different audiences and services, increase conversion volume and make more efficient use of advertising budgets. The priority was acquisition efficiency and conversion results rather than impressions alone.

Comparison: 14 Nov 2021–15 Nov 2023 versus 16 Nov 2023–16 Nov 2025.

The aggregated Google Ads report for 11 projects shows:

  • 123,024.89 all conversions, up 259.53% versus the preceding period.
  • $118,594.79 in ad spend, up 42.79%; conversions grew considerably faster than spending.
  • $0.96 cost per conversion, down 60.29%.
  • 1,498,554 clicks, up 109.38%, while impressions fell 22.94%.
  • 5.23% CTR, up 171.70%; $0.08 average CPC, down 31.80%.
  • 4.81% all-conversion rate, up 71.37%.

The report uses Google Ads All conv., which can include multiple conversion action types. It should not automatically be interpreted as the number of unique enquiries or customers.

Overall efficiency: Over the two-year period, all conversions increased 3.6-fold while spend rose about 1.43-fold. Average cost per conversion fell by more than half.
