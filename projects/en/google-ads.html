Objective

Improve advertising efficiency for 11 projects in recruitment, transportation, Romanian citizenship and tax refunds. The goal was to grow conversions without a proportionate increase in advertising spend.

Interpreting the results

  • All conversions grew by 259.53% while spend rose by 42.79%, and aggregate cost per conversion fell by 60.29%.
  • CTR increased by 171.70%, average CPC fell by 31.80%, and clicks increased by 109.38%.
  • Impressions fell by 22.94%. Fewer impressions alone do not prove improved traffic quality.

Important: Google Ads reports «All conv.», which may combine several types of conversion actions and include fractional attribution. Therefore, 123,024.89 all conversions do not necessarily mean the same number of leads or customers. These are historical aggregate metrics, not proof of a specific tactic's causal effect.
