MobileStyles, USA

Category Booking App / Beauty Services
Period 2020 — 2023
Location USA (English-speaking segment)

SEO for a beauty booking service in the US. I expanded the structure, worked on service pages and the blog, and addressed technical issues. Baseline traffic grew from roughly 1,500 to over 5,000 sessions, with more than 6,900 ranking keywords.

Data note: metrics refer to the period and sources shown in this case study. Rankings, impressions and traffic alone do not establish the number of enquiries or sales.

🎯

Increase the number of registered masters and clients, as well as app downloads. Ensure organic traffic growth and increase brand visibility in the English-speaking market.
