ExportPortal, Global

Category International B2B Platform / Import-Export
Period 2020 — 2023
Location Global (international market)

SEO for an international B2B platform: a technical audit, catalogue structure and broader keyword coverage. Monthly traffic grew from roughly 2,000 to over 10,000 sessions, with more than 3,100 ranking keywords in the report.

Data note: metrics refer to the period and sources shown in this case study. Rankings, impressions and traffic alone do not establish the number of enquiries or sales.

🎯

Challenge

Bring the website in line with current SEO guidelines, attract organic traffic, increase brand visibility, and increase registrations from exporters, importers, and manufacturers at the international level.

What Was Done
